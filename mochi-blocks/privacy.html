Mochi Blocks – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026 · Developer: Agro Games, Sri Lanka

This policy explains what information the Android game Mochi Blocks (“the game”) collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short because the game collects very little.

1. Information we do not collect

The game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location.

2. Information stored only on your device

Your best score, current game, sound and vibration settings, and whether you finished the tutorial are saved in a file on your device. This file is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

3. Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • ad interactions such as views and taps.

Depending on your choices, ads may be personalised. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice at any time from the game’s Settings. Anywhere, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Learn how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Crash and performance data

If the game crashes, Google Play may share anonymous crash reports with us through the Google Play Console. These do not identify you.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Security and retention

We do not run our own servers for the game and do not hold your personal data. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google’s own retention policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, most requests are handled through your device settings or directly with Google; we are happy to help if you contact us.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
